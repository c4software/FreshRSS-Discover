First public release.

• All your FreshRSS subscriptions in one endless vertical stream, sources interleaved.
• Two modes over the same feed: List, or Swipe one article at a time.
• Reading is scrolling: articles visible long enough become read, with adjustable thresholds.
• Read statuses sent back to your server, queued and retried after an outage.
• Local cache readable offline, automatic purge, daily reading reminder.
• Talks only to your server: no telemetry, no third party, no background sync.
